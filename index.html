How this works
Session-based, not date-based. Rest days are your choice. Sessions alternate Day A (Push, Legs, Core) and Day B (Pull, Core, Warmup).
Auto-progression. Each exercise starts near your current ability and adds a small increment every 2 successful sessions, building up to a ceiling — then switches to the next, harder variation. No manual decisions.
Deload. 3 full misses in a row on a stage automatically triggers one reduced-load session (target cut ~20%) to shed fatigue, then resumes normal progression immediately after — pass or fail.
